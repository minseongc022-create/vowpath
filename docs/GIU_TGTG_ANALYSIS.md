# GIU vs Too Good To Go — compressed for HCMC

## Benchmark apps

| App | What we borrow | What we beat |
|-----|----------------|--------------|
| **Too Good To Go** | Surprise bags, map discover, favorites+alerts, impact, escrow-like trust | Anytime windows (not fixed golden hour), map-first home, in-map route+ETA, VN/KO + VietQR/MoMo |
| **Olio / Karma** | Neighbor rescue urgency | Faster merchant publish + pickup confirm |
| **Grab / Gojek food** | Directions from me, live ETA feel | Food-waste mission + surprise pricing |
