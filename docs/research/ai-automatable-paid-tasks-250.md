# 250+ Rule-Based Paid Tasks Potentially AI-Automatable

**Compiled:** 2026-07-26  
**Scope:** Tasks real people are paid to do repeatedly today (daily/weekly). Rule-based / checklist-heavy. Not invented startup ideas.  
**Format:** `#. Task | Who does it | Frequency | Cost signal | Evidence`

### Evidence key
| Code | Source |
|------|--------|
| **BLS** | U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook / OEWS (2023–2024) |
| **UW** | Upwork hire/services categories & VA role pages |
| **SVC** | Known commercial service / agency category (e.g. Ruby Receptionists, RCM vendors) |
| **SaaS** | Established product category with paying customers doing/replacing this work |

### Wage & rate anchors used below
- Receptionists (BLS 43-4171): median **$37,230** / ~$17.90/hr (2024)
- Customer service reps (BLS): median **$42,830** / ~$20.59/hr (2024)
- General office clerks (BLS): median **$43,630**
- Bookkeeping clerks (BLS): median **$49,210** / ~$23.66/hr (2024)
- Billing & posting clerks (BLS OEWS): median ~**$45,590**
- Medical secretaries (BLS 43-6013): ~850k jobs; typical clinic admin **$35–50k**
- Medical records specialists (BLS): median **$50,250**
- Paralegals / legal assistants (BLS): median **$61,010**
- Upwork VAs: commonly **$10–20/hr** (admin); offshore often **$5–15/hr**
- Live answering (Ruby-class): ~**$245–$1,700+/mo** by minute tier
- Prior-auth / authorization specialists: market ~**$34–62k** (job-market medians ~$47–49k)

Frequencies are **typical busy SMB / mid-market shop** ranges (not universal constants).

## Notes for automation buyers / builders

1. **Highest repetition + clearest rules** tend to cluster in: eligibility/prior-auth status chase, appointment reminders/no-show backfill, inbox FAQ triage, AR dunning, CRM stage logging, L1 password/order-status, and field-service dispatch texts.  
2. **Already productized** (strong SaaS/SVC evidence): live answering, appointment reminders, AP OCR, sales engagement cadences, RCM eligibility, field-service dispatch, property-mgmt work orders.  
3. **Labor cost floor:** offshore VA **$5–15/hr** and domestic VA **$10–20/hr** (Upwork) set the practical price ceiling for pure admin automation unless quality, compliance (HIPAA/TCPA), or latency justifies more.  
4. **BLS headcount anchors** show these are not niche: ~1.0M receptionists, ~2.8M CSRs, ~1.6M bookkeeping clerks, ~850k medical secretaries, ~195k medical records specialists — large pools doing repetitive admin daily.
